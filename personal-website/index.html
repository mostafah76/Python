Who I Am

About Me

I am a professor of Smart Systems Engineering with deep expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning. My academic career is driven by a commitment to advancing intelligent, adaptive systems that can solve real-world engineering challenges.

My teaching philosophy centers on connecting theoretical foundations with practical implementation — ensuring students not only understand the "why" but also master the "how" of modern engineering and computing.

Beyond the classroom, my research explores the intersection of intelligent control systems, neural computing, and hardware-software co-design — areas that define the future of smart technology.
